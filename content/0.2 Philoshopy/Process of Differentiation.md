> [!Question] AI ASKS: The Problem of Singular Universals (The Boundary Case)
>  If differentiation strictly requires a contrast against a background, how does the human mind differentiate the _entire physical universe_ as a single concept? Because there is no ontological "non-universe" or physical background to contrast it against, the process of differentiation faces a structural limit at the absolute boundary of metaphysics. Does the mind differentiate the universe by contrasting it with the _absence_ of existence (nothingness)—which is an epistemological blank, not an ontological existent—or does it differentiate it internally by contrasting its constituent parts?
