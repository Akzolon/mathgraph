### Definition

A [[Tropism|goal-orinted]] cognitive process of isolating [[Percept|a percept]], [[Concept|a concept]] or [[Group|a category/set]] from others based on an [[Perceptual Recognition|implicitly recognized]] [[Identity]] or an [[Process of conceptual identification|explicitly identified]] [[property]].

### Questions

> [!Question] Singular Universals
>  If differentiation strictly requires a contrast against a background, how does the human mind differentiate the _entire physical universe_ as a single concept? Because there is no ontological "non-universe" or physical background to contrast it against, the process of differentiation faces a structural limit at the boundary of the Identified Universe. Does the mind differentiate the universe by contrasting it with the _absence_ of existence (nothingness)—which is an epistemological blank, not an ontological existent—or does it differentiate it internally by contrasting its constituent parts?

> [!success] My Answer
> We differentiate the ontological "Identified Universe" set against any valid combination of the "Identified Universe" set
