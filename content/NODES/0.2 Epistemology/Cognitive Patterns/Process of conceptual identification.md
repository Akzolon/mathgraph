### Definition

The [[(Human) Action|purposeful]] process of identifying [[Percept|a percept]], [[Abstraction|an abstraction]], [[Relation|a relation]] or [[Function|an operation]] by [[Process of Differentiation|differentiating]] relevant [[Property|aspects of identity]] through [[measurment-omission|omitting irrelevant measurements]] and [[Process of Integration|integrating]] them into a [[cognitive procedure]] according to the [[Conceptual Common Denominator]].
