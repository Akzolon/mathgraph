### Definition

A [[Common property|common property]] of a [[Group|set]] which describes the amount of elements the [[Group|set]] contains.

### Math

Take an example [[Group|set]]:

$\{Dog, Cat, Mouse\}$

[[Process of conceptual identification|Generalize]] the [[Entity|elements]] according to their [[Common property]]:

$\{x_1, x_2, x_3\}$

The [[Commonality|commonly]] [[Process of conceptual identification|generalized]] set of $\{x_n\}$ thus gives us information on:

- The [[Natural Numbers|number]] of [[Entity|elements]] contained in the [[Group|set]]
