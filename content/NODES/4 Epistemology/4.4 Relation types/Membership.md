### Definition

A [[Relation]] of an [[Structured Existent|Existent]] belonging in a [[Group|set]]. 

### Elaboration

"$\in$" is the symbol that denotes Membership.

$x\in\{x,y,z\}$ means:

x is a member of an "x,y,z" set.
