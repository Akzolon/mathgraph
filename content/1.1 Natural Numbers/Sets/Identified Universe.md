### Definition

A [[Cardinal Finity|finite]] [[Group|set]] of all [[Perceptual Recognition|implicitly]] or [[Process of conceptual identification|explicitly]] identified [[Entity|entities]].

### Math

Symbols: $\mathbb{D}$ , $\mathcal I$

Approximated to contain maximally $10^{10^{121}}$ elements at a single [[Moment|unit of time]], which would include:
- Every [[property-set]]
- Every [[dependency]]
- Every [[causal event]]

Approximated to have roughly $10^{10^{241}}$ non-alternating combinations.
