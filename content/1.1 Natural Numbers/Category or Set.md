---
title: Category / Set
---

A [[Abstraction|mental integration]] of [[Object or Element|objects/elements]] sharing a relevant aspect of [[Identity|Identity]]. 
A category or set focuses primarily on [[Process of element or object Differentiation|differentiation]] of particular [[Object or Element|objects/elements]] it subsumes.

An example set:

$\{Dog, Cat, Mouse\}$

---

In the example set, the set is composed of two parts:

a)
- the elements that compose the set

$Dog, Cat, Mouse$

b)
- the elements that denote the set

$\{\}$

---

We can then study the set in the said two ways.

a)
- Studying the set [[Collective nature of a set|through the nature of its constituents]], what can we infer from it?

Questions like: 
- What kind of [[Object or Element|entities]] compose this set or category?
- Are there [[Identity|distinguishing characteristics]] which warrants their grouping?
- Do they fit a wider category?

are questions about the [[Collective nature of a set|collective nature of a set]].


b)
- Studying the set [[Common nature of a set|as a single whole]], what can we infer from it?

Questions like: 
- How many members compose this set or category?
- Is the aforementioned set finite?
- Is the aforementioned set empty?

are questions about the [[Common nature of a set|common nature of a set]].
